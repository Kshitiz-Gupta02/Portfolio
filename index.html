Godrej Consumer Products · 11-Year Review

DuPont ROE Decomposition & Balance Sheet Deleveraging

Deconstructed Return on Equity into Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier. Revealed that healthy ~19–24% ROE was sustained entirely by operating discipline, while GCPL progressively paid down debt, dropping D/E to 0.00x and Equity Multiplier from 1.42x to 1.13x.

Key Takeaway: Growth funded organically through retained earnings without financial leverage artificially boosting shareholder return.
