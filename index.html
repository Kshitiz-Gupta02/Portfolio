DOM208 · Operations Research

Ambulance Routing via Graph Theory

Shaving real minutes off ambulance response times using shortest-path math.

Problem

Ambulance routing usually comes down to driver instinct, not math — and minutes matter.

Approach

Modelled the road network as a weighted directed graph and ran Dijkstra's algorithm for onward and return legs.

Outcome

Cut a 20-minute round trip to 18 on a sample network — a 10% time saving.
