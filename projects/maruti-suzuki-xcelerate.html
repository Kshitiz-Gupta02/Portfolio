2. Market Entry Strategy: Target Customer, Launch States, and GTM

Strategic components formulated in the XCELerate Round 3 submission deck:

Pillar 1
Target Customer Profile

The target customer is the mid-range "ideal customer", first-time car buyers seeking feature-rich, comfortable vehicles.

Pillar 2
Launch State Sequencing

Delhi and Karnataka were recommended as launch states, backed by charging-station density data.

Pillar 3
Go-To-Market Concept

The go-to-market idea was "Maruti Suzuki eBharat", positioned on affordability and low running cost, with range-anxiety myth-busting through test drives and a social/UGC campaign (#eBharatRoadTrip).

Deep Dive: Strategy Formulation & Campaign Design (Click to expand)

The strategy targeted first-time car buyers looking for feature-rich, comfortable vehicles in the mid-range segment, supported by initial rollouts in Delhi and Karnataka where charging infrastructure was densest.

The "Maruti Suzuki eBharat" brand campaign paired test-drive activations to dispel range anxiety with an organic user-generated content campaign (#eBharatRoadTrip) emphasizing affordability and operating economics.
