Daikoku: Student Builder Marketplace

A student builder marketplace connecting student talent with early-stage startups. Co-founder from June 2022 to December 2022. Incubated at SNU Atal Incubation Centre from August 2022.

Executive Summary

The Problem
Early-stage startups struggled to evaluate junior talent without practical proof of work, while students lacked structured access to startup projects.
My Role
Co-Founder, Operations, Strategy & Marketing Lead across 4 co-founders (Ops and Strategy, Web Dev, XR/Unity, Ops and Marketing).
Core Tools
B2B Outbound
Primary Outcome
Incubated at SNU Atal Incubation Centre from August 2022. Customer discovery with 21 startups (13 meetings scheduled, 62% conversion rate). Early revenue of Rs 23,000 from a website-development engagement for one of our outreach targets. This figure is directional, as the books were not formally closed after the team's exit.

1. Student Builder Marketplace Concept

Daikoku was designed as a student builder marketplace connecting student talent with early-stage startups across technical and operational domains.

MARKETPLACE ARCHITECTURE
STUDENT PLATFORM
Student Portal

A student portal (Google and GitHub sign-in, project listings, embedded workspace, gamified rewards).

HIRING INTERFACE
Swipe Mode

Swipe Mode (hiring interface where startup HRs filter and message pre-evaluated candidates, backed by SME project ratings and employer verification).

STARTUP PLATFORM
Tiered SaaS Toolkit

A tiered SaaS toolkit for startups.

2. Customer Discovery, Survey Results & Early Revenue

Validation metrics across startup outbound discovery, student surveys, and initial commercial traction:

STARTUP DISCOVERY MEETINGS
13 of 21 (62%)

13 of 21 startups converted into scheduled meetings.

EARLY REVENUE
Rs 23,000

Early revenue of Rs 23,000 from a website-development engagement for one of our outreach targets. This figure is directional, as the books were not formally closed after the team's exit.

STUDENT SURVEY (53 STUDENTS)
79.2% Projects

79.2% were willing to do projects. 60.4% preferred big companies over startups (39.6% startups). About 79% were excited about VR training (50.9% very excited, 28.3% excited).
