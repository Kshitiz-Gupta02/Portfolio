Strategy & Consulting Growth Central VC Market Research · 2021

Competitor Landscape — 214 Platforms Mapped

Leading a 7-person research team to map the online skill-assessment and hiring-assessment space, ahead of Growth Central VC's decision on what to build next.

RoleManagement Intern, led 7-person team
Timeline3 weeks (vs. a 6-week allocation)
MethodCritical Path Method (CPM)

Context

Growth Central VC had entered the market only a few months earlier with a single product — skill assessment tests and certificates. With that first product live, leadership was deciding what to launch next, and needed a structured view of the competitive landscape to make that call. There was no existing research base to work from.

My Role

I led a team of 7 research interns end-to-end — set the research questions, assigned and guided what each researcher should investigate, managed the project timeline using the Critical Path Method, and synthesized the team's findings into a final recommendation.

Approach

We defined 3 core research questions — demand size, price sensitivity, and competitive gaps — and combined secondary research (industry reports, competitor pricing, trade data) with primary research (surveys of 30+ existing customers). The team mapped 214 competitors in total: 163 in certificate assessments and 51 in hiring assessments, then built a focused comparison framework benchmarking 5 key platforms on price, quality, and distribution. We applied the same strengths/weaknesses lens to Growth Central VC itself, not just competitors, so the recommendation reflected our own starting position too.

Price vs. Credibility Positioning

No incumbent combined a low price point with high perceived credibility — Coursera and edX earned trust at a premium price, Udemy and Growth Central VC sat on the low-cost, low-trust end. That open quadrant directly informed the go/no-go recommendation on Growth Central VC's next product.

Outcome

We delivered the full 214-competitor landscape map in 3 weeks — half the allocated 6-week timeline. Benchmarking the top 5 platforms surfaced a clear open gap in the market, which directly informed the team's next-product recommendation to leadership. Off the back of this project, the founder moved me directly onto Growth Central VC's Core Team.
