Product Management Course Design

Hiring four industry experts to build self-paced courses, while personally designing and teaching a live, cohort-based Product Management program from scratch.

RoleOwned course layout, operations & tech platform
TimelinePlanning Nov, 4 courses live by Jan, PM course live Feb

Context

As part of overseeing Growth Central VC's product lines, I was tasked with building and launching a new education product from the ground up — bringing in outside domain expertise while also creating a flagship course of my own.

The 4 Expert-Led Courses

I hired 4 experts with 10+ years of experience each, across Finance, Marketing, Computer Science, and Cyber Security. Each authored their own curriculum and recorded their own video content, structured as self-paced 2-month courses with a weekly live doubt-clearing class to keep engagement high. Post-course internship placement support was built in as a core benefit, not an afterthought.

The PM Course (Self-Designed)

While the 4 expert courses were being built, I designed and taught a fundamentally different kind of course myself — live-only, cohort-based, and built around a hands-on internship rather than recorded content. Teaching thesis: anyone can manage a team or a product — what makes a Product Manager specifically is understanding the product at its core and driving its improvement, whether judged from the company's perspective or the customer's.

8-Week Curriculum Map

WEEKS 1–2 Theory Foundations Core management concepts and types.
→
WEEKS 2–7 Live Management Internship Mentees work as management interns, leading a real team for 6 weeks. Weekly assignments run alongside (Weeks 3–7).
→
WEEKS 7–8 Theory + Exams Consolidation and assessment.

Cohort of 10 handpicked mentees, every 3 months — a 2-month course plus a 1-month break to refine materials and select the next cohort.
