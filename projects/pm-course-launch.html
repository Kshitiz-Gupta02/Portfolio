Executive Summary

The Problem
Growth Central VC needed to expand beyond skill certifications by launching a complete educational product line from scratch.
My Role
Core Team Member: owned operations and tech platform, hired and onboarded 4 industry experts, and personally designed, built, and taught the PM program.
Core Tools
Curriculum Design, Cohort Mentorship, Operations Management, Figma & Notion
Primary Outcome
Generated ₹3,00,000 in first-quarter revenue across the new product line: ₹2,50,000 from 4 expert courses plus ₹50,000 from the PM program (3 cohorts of 10 mentees).

1. PM Program Cohort Structure (8-Week Cycle)

A new PM cohort started every month from January. Each cohort ran 8 weeks with 10 mentees. I personally ran 3 cohorts (30 mentees) before handing it off. The ₹50,000 PM revenue came from those 3 cohorts in the first quarter.

8-WEEK COHORT CYCLE (10 MENTEES PER COHORT)
Weeks 1-2 Theory Foundations: Management fundamentals, organizational types, and team leadership
Weeks 2-7 Live Management Internship: Mentees work as management interns at the company and lead their own team for 6 weeks, with weekly assignments
Weeks 7-8 Return to Theory and Exams: Closing synthesis, final reviews, and program wrap-up

Figure 1: 8-week cycle with overlapping theory and live management internship phases.

The 4 expert-led courses (Finance, Marketing, Computer Science, and Cyber Security) were structured as self-paced 2-month courses with weekly live doubt-clearing sessions, achieving an 85% completion rate.
