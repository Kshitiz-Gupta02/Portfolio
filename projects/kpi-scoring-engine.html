KPI Scoring Engine: Three Iterations

Designed a balanced multi-variable scoring model that aligned sourcing volume, shortlisting selectivity, and final hiring targets: V1 for the departments I oversaw, V2 and V3 for the HR teams only (3 HR teams, 12 groups, 60 recruiters).

Executive Summary

The Problem
Primitive task counting incentivized low-effort volume over quality, creating misaligned incentives and team friction.
My Role
Core Team Member: authored BRD, 13 user stories, mapped ERDs, executed 22 UAT test cases, and built PostgreSQL to Power BI pipeline.
Core Tools
Excel, Power BI, PostgreSQL, DBeaver, JIRA, draw.io
Primary Outcome
V1 for the departments I oversaw, V2 and V3 for the HR teams only (3 HR teams, 12 groups, 60 recruiters).

1. Business Analysis & System Lifecycle

Timeline: V1 built August 2021, V2 built March 2022, V3 started June 2022 and live August 2022. The scoring model was documented through formal Business Requirements Documents (BRDs), 13 user stories, and Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERDs). I structured 22 User Acceptance Testing (UAT) test cases tracked in JIRA to catch and fix edge case scoring defects before company-wide release.

2. The V3 Scoring Formula: Balancing Volume and Quality

Designed over 3 iterations: V1 for the departments I oversaw, V2 and V3 for the HR teams only (3 HR teams, 12 groups, 60 recruiters), replacing a rigid volume-based system with a balanced, gamified model that aligned sourcing volume with hiring quality.

FINAL PRODUCTION FORMULA
Score = 5(A) + 2(B) - 6(C) + 10(D) - 4(E)
+5 pts: Job Ads (A)
+2 pts: Sourced (B)
-6 pts: Shortlisted (C)
+10 pts: Interview (D)
-4 pts: Rejection (E)
