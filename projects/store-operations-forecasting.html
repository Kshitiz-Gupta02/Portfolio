Executive Summary

The Problem
Unstructured customer handling created 20-minute customer interaction delays and seasonal replenishment bottlenecks during seasonal demand spikes.
My Role
Managed store and warehouse operations for a 15-person team (stock checks, replenishment from 6 vendors, packing compliance), conducted a time-and-motion study, and built a forecasting prototype.
Core Tools
Excel, Time-and-Motion Study
Primary Outcome
Customer interaction time cut from 20 to 12-13 minutes, basket size raised from 2-3 to 4-5 products, and replenishment synchronized across 6 vendors.
