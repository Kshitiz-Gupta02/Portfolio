Godrej Consumer Products: 11-Year DuPont ROE Analysis

Deconstructed 11 years of standalone financial reports (FY12 to FY22) to evaluate Return on Equity across operational profitability, asset turnover, and capital structure.

Executive Summary

The Problem
Evaluating Godrej Consumer Products' historical Return on Equity drivers across 11 fiscal years (FY12 to FY22).
My Role
Deconstructed 11 years of standalone financial reports (FY12 to FY22), ran 3-step DuPont decomposition, and isolated one-off tax events.
Core Tools
DuPont Ratio Modeling
Primary Outcome
ROE ranged between roughly 18.5% and 23.9% in every year except FY2019 (35.6%, one-off tax year); Total Asset Turnover declined from 0.85x to 0.79x while Fixed Asset Turnover improved from 2.55x to 4.78x.

1. 3-Step DuPont Decomposition Model

DuPont analysis splits Return on Equity into three distinct operational levers: Profitability (Net Profit Margin), Asset Efficiency (Total Asset Turnover), and Financial Leverage (Equity Multiplier). This approach ensures capital efficiency is evaluated without masking leverage risk.

DUPONT IDENTITY
ROE = Net Profit Margin x Asset Turnover x Equity Multiplier

Where Net Profit Margin = Net Income / Revenue, Asset Turnover = Revenue / Total Assets, and Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Shareholders Equity.
