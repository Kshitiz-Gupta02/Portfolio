The Problem

Team Member in a 4-person team (issue-tree diagnostics, financial calculations and growth strategy).
Core Tools
MECE issue trees, financial and utilization calculations
Primary Outcome
Cleared Round 1 of 3 (MCQ assessment plus issue-tree submission), submitted a Round 2 entry, and was not selected for Round 3.

1. Four Root Causes of Stagnation

The Round 2 executive summary established four primary drivers behind the stagnation of the HF business:

01. MARKET DRIVER
Raw Material Import Dependency

100% import-dependent raw material, with fluorspar supply disruption raising supplier bargaining power.

02. COMPANY DRIVER
Volatile Capacity Utilization

Volatile capacity utilization of 37% to 64% (FY20 to FY22) at a single plant.

03. COMPANY DRIVER
Zero Pricing Flexibility

$0 pricing flexibility, since HF prices have been flat industry-wide for a decade.

04. MARKET & COMPANY
Geographic Concentration

Distribution concentrated in South India (60% of sales) with a 10% new-customer rate, versus competitors at 50% to 80%.

Deep Dive: Diagnostic Findings & Root Cause Analysis (Click to expand)

FluorCo, a roughly 50-year-old hydrofluoric acid manufacturer, had about 40% overall business CAGR over three years, but its HF business was only a third of sales and had stagnated.

The root causes identified in Round 2 were: 100% import-dependent raw material with fluorspar supply disruptions, volatile single-plant capacity utilization of 37% to 64% from FY20 to FY22, $0 pricing flexibility due to flat industry prices, and distribution concentrated in South India (60% of sales) with a 10% new-customer rate versus 50% to 80% for competitors.
