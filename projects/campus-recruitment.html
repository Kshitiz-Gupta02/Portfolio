Growth Central VC 33% part-time conversion (5 of 15 interns)

Campus Recruitment & Structured Interview Pipeline

Led end-to-end campus recruitment across 5 institutions to build the internship pipeline: sourced 100+ applicants, interviewed 60+ candidates, and onboarded 15 interns with 5 converting to part-time roles (33% conversion rate).

Executive Summary

The Problem
Placement cells from 5 institutions proactively reached out to partner, requiring a structured campus pipeline rather than reactive hiring.
My Role
Core Team Member: defined role requirements and interview criteria, ran drives with a rotating pair of HR trainees, and managed the full hiring funnel.
Core Tools
Interview criteria design, Campus partnerships, Funnel management
Primary Outcome
Onboarded 15 interns across Management, Sales, Marketing, and HR functions. 5 of 15 interns converted to part-time roles after internship completion, a 33% conversion rate.

1. Hiring Funnel Progression

Worked with placement cells at 5 institutions (LPU, KMC, DDUC, DTU E-Cell and A₹thaShastra, the economics club of IIM Rohtak), timing the funnel backwards from the semester calendar: campus invites from March, interviews by May, and internships June to August 2022.
