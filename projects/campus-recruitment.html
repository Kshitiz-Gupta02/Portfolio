Operations & Product Growth Central VC Summer 2022

Campus Recruitment & Internship Hiring

Building Growth Central VC's internship pipeline across 6 campus partnerships — defining hiring criteria, running the full funnel, and training new HR trainees along the way.

RoleCore Team Member — personally led the function
Scope6 campus partnerships

Context

As a Core Team Member overseeing most of Growth Central VC's departments, I was responsible for hiring across multiple open roles heading into early 2022. Placement cells from 6 institutions proactively reached out to partner on internship fairs, creating an opportunity to build a structured campus pipeline rather than hiring reactively.

My Role

I personally defined which roles to hire for and set the interview criteria, then led execution end-to-end alongside a rotating pair of HR trainees for each campus drive.

Approach

I coordinated with placement cells across 6 institutions — LPU, KMC, DDUC, ArthaShastra (Economics Club, IIM Rohtak), and the E-Cell of DTU — to schedule and run internship fairs, and defined the roles and interview criteria for openings across Management, Sales, Marketing, and HR. I managed the full hiring funnel — sourcing, screening, interviews, onboarding — and used each campus drive as a live training ground, hand-picking and training 2 HR trainees per recruitment cycle for hands-on screening and interviewing experience under supervision.

The Hiring Funnel

4 of 12 interns converted to part-time roles after internship completion — a 33% conversion rate, across Management, Sales, Marketing, and HR functions.
