Operations & Product Gyasi Ram and Sons Retail Operations

Time and Motion Study

Studying how a retail and wholesale floor actually worked, then fixing the parts that were slowing it down.

RoleAssistant Store Manager
Scope15-person team, 6 supplier vendors, 50-SKU catalog

Context

At Gyasi Ram and Sons, an established retail and wholesale distributor in Delhi, operational efficiency was hindered by slow customer handling and fulfilment backlogs during peak seasons. As Assistant Store Manager, I managed daily operations across a 15-person team and 6 supplier vendors.

Problem

Customer interaction time was running around 20 minutes with small average basket sizes of 2–3 items, and unstudied warehouse bottlenecks caused order backlogs whenever seasonal demand surged.

Approach

I ran the business's first formal time-and-motion study on packing and fulfilment workflows, analysing throughput data across the full 50-SKU netting catalog. I used that data to build a seasonal inventory forecasting model that aligned stock replenishment with sales cycles, and introduced structured product-education methods for sales staff so customers could navigate technical specifications faster.

Before vs. After

Outcome

Average customer interaction time dropped from 20 minutes to 12–13 minutes while average basket size nearly doubled, from 2–3 items to 4–5 items per visit. The seasonal forecasting model eliminated the fulfilment delays that had previously hit the warehouse during demand spikes.
