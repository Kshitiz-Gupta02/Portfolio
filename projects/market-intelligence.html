Growth Central VC 200+ Competitors Mapped (3 Weeks)

Competitor Intelligence: 200+ Competitors Mapped

Led a 7-person research team to map 200+ competitors across course learning and skill assessment & certification in a 3-week sprint using the Critical Path Method, benchmarking 5 key platforms on price, quality, and distribution.

Executive Summary

The Problem
Growth Central VC had entered the market with skill assessment tests and needed a structured competitor landscape to decide which product line to build next.
My Role
Product Management Intern: led a team of 7 research interns end-to-end, managed the timeline using the Critical Path Method, and synthesized findings into a final recommendation.
Core Tools
Critical Path Method, competitor comparison framework
Primary Outcome
Mapped 200+ competitors in 3 weeks. Found few major competitors combined low price with high perceived credibility, leading directly to launching course learning.

1. Project Scheduling via Critical Path Method

Managed the research sprint using the Critical Path Method (CPM) for project scheduling and task sequencing, completing the landscape mapping in 3 weeks (half the allocated 6-week timeline).

2. Five-Platform Benchmark: Price, Quality, and Distribution

SYNTHESIZED MARKET FINDING

Primary research surveyed 30+ existing customers, combined with secondary research (industry reports, competitor pricing, trade data). The chart finding showed that few major competitors combined low price with high perceived credibility, leading directly to launching course learning as the next product line.
